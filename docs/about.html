About

Words Matter

Book of Mormon Explorer is a website that helps people study the Book of Mormon using data analysis and interactive visual tools. It focuses on the different voices and speakers in the book—how they teach, how they use language, and how their ideas connect.

The goal of this site is to help readers discover patterns in the text that are hard to see through reading alone.

Why It Was Created

The Book of Mormon contains many speakers—prophets, leaders, missionaries, and teachers. Each one shares the gospel in a slightly different way.

Book of Mormon Explorer explores these differences using stylometry, the study of writing style. Stylometry looks at patterns in how people use words to better understand how different speakers communicate.

The research behind this site builds on the work of Paul J. Fields, who studied language patterns among Book of Mormon speakers. To learn more, you can read about Dr. Fields' study, watch a 12-minute interview with Dr. Fields, or view Book of Mormon Voices, an hour-long presentation give by Dr. Fields.

What You Can Do on This Site

Book of Mormon Explorer offers two main ways to study the Book of Mormon:

Articles

Short studies explore the words, teaching patterns, and ideas used by 37 different speakers in the Book of Mormon. These articles show how speakers use language and how later speakers echo or build on earlier teachings. You can find these in the People, Echoes, and Concepts sections.

Interactive Tools

Several visual tools help you explore the text in new ways. They are split into Basic Tools (also found on the home page and in People) and Advanced Tools (found at the links below):

Basic Tools

Timeline - Shows when speakers lived and major events in their lives.

Word frequencies - Displays the words each speaker uses most often.

Similar Verse Finder - Finds verses with similar meaning.

Connections - Maps relationships between speakers, including family, adversaries, and spiritual communication.

Advanced Tools

Similar Topic Diagram - Groups passages that discuss similar ideas.

Semantic Map - Plots every verse based on similarity of meaning.

Conversation Network - Shows who speaks to whom in the text.

Stylo XR - Visualize speaker style and voice.

A New Way to Study the Book of Mormon

By combining scripture study with modern data tools, Book of Mormon Explorer helps readers see the structure, voices, and connections within the Book of Mormon in a clear and visual way.
